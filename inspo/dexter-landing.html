✦ the problem

The work around the work

Freelancers don't just do the project — they carry it. Every decision, preference, and half-remembered comment lives somewhere different, and somewhere is rarely the same place twice.

💬

Client messages

Feedback, approvals, and changes of mind, spread across threads.

📁

Files

Drafts, briefs, and assets — with no record of what they meant at the time.

📝

Notes

Quick thoughts jotted down and never looked at again.

🗓️

Decisions & deadlines

Agreed once, remembered loosely, and never written down properly.

✦ what it is

A project-aware collaborator

Dexter reads the messy material and builds a living project dossier — a structured record of where things stand, what changed, what was agreed, and what needs attention.

Most tools remember data.
This one remembers context.

✦ how it works

Four pillars, one dossier

Everything Dexter does feeds back into a single living record of the project — and out again as clear, actionable next steps.

✦ dashboard

Project dashboard

Status, phase, deadlines, setbacks, and recent updates — at a glance, for every project you're running.

✦ dossier

Living project dossier

Timeline, scope, client preferences, decisions, feedback, and open questions — kept current, automatically.

✦ triage

Task triage

Work sorted into what you decide, what Dexter can prepare, and what just needs to happen quietly in the background.

✦ briefing

Progress updates

A clear answer to "what changed, what's blocked, and what's next" — whenever you need it.
